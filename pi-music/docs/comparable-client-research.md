# Comparable Music Client Research

## Scope and method

This is a read-only product and architecture comparison. It reviews public documentation and repositories only; no third-party code, extractors, plugins, installers, or binaries are executed. Pi-Music will use the findings to define original product requirements, not to copy a client or bypass a provider.

## Initial findings

| Client | Publicly documented strengths | Architecture lesson for Pi-Music | Boundary Pi-Music will keep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Spotube** | Cross-platform application with metadata/audio separation, time-synced lyrics, tagged downloads, local playback control, and a community plugin model; it declares BSD-4-Clause licensing and no telemetry.[1] | A mature client needs clear separation of discovery, queue, playback, lyrics, persistence, and releases. | Do not adopt its plugin/extractor or cross-source stream-matching model. | **Nuclear** | Windows, macOS, and Linux support; documented search, artist/album browsing, queue controls, favorites, playlists, themes, keyboard shortcuts, localization, auto-updates, and a plugin architecture; it describes itself as ad-free and tracking-free.[2] | Treat queue management, library/favorites, keyboard control, localization, error states, and release maintenance as first-class product systems, not add-ons. | Do not adopt a plugin store or “stream from any source” model. Pi-Music will expose one first-party YouTube route. |
| **Strawberry** | GPL-3.0 collection manager in C++/Qt with advanced, smart, and dynamic playlists; native notifications and remote control; broad format support; and lyric lookup from several providers, including commercial and unofficial sources.[3] | Build playlists, queue behaviour, notifications, media controls, and no-lyrics states as durable app systems. A lyric panel must handle timing/source failure gracefully. | Its local-collection and broad unlicensed lyric-source approach do not fit Pi-Music’s online-only, provider-approved product boundary. |

| **Moosync** | A cross-platform customizable desktop player documenting local files, Spotify integration, lyric lookup, Last.fm scrobbling, mixed-provider playlists, themes, and an extension API.[8] | Favor explicit account, loading, search, and lyrics states; its public TODO also reinforces the importance of not showing technical implementation detail to listeners. | Do not adopt its mixed-provider playlists, Spotify integration, or extension-runtime model. Pi-Music will keep one official YouTube route and a first-party product surface. |

## Early Pi-Music implication

The comparable products confirm that a complete player is more than a playback button. Pi-Music needs a durable screen system for search, result selection, now playing, lyrics, online playlists, saved music, account consent, empty/loading/error states, keyboard/media controls, privacy surfaces, and cross-platform releases. The next research entries will examine specialized local-library, self-hosted, commercial-polish, and YouTube-specific patterns before implementation resumes.

## Original Pi-Music blueprint

Pi-Music will be a **YouTube-only native desktop listening room** rather than a generic multi-provider aggregator. The foundation is a Tauri desktop shell with an original retro hi-fi interface, first-party screen state, and an official visible YouTube player. Its product structure will consist of seven real rooms: **Listen**, **Search**, **Results**, **Now Playing**, **Words**, **Playlists**, and **Settings/Privacy**.

The account flow must begin as disconnected and make the user’s next action clear. After an explicit Google authorization, the app can show authorized YouTube discovery and playlist information through the applicable official interfaces; the player panel will remain visibly embedded and will drive its status from documented player events. A failed login, unavailable video, consent cancellation, autoplay block, or unavailable content must have a designed recovery state rather than silently failing.[7]

The **Words** room will not be built from scraped text or simulated lines. It is gated behind a legitimate commercial lyrics relationship, a verified recording match, and a timing feed synchronized with real player progress. Until then, its polished state says that words are unavailable for the selected recording. This is a feature-quality decision learned from clients that expose lyrics as a distinct subsystem, not a decorative detail.[1] [3] [4] [8]

The distinctive Pi-Music advantage will be its tactile listening-room UI: warm paper and walnut materials, amber receiver cues, readable liner-note typography, small handmade controls, and carefully written empty/error states. It must remain clearly different from the layouts and branding of each researched product. The mature-client benchmark is feature depth and operational clarity—not visual cloning.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

Pi-Music will not run third-party source plugins, scrape or inject into YouTube Music, resolve hidden direct streams, bypass advertisements or subscription controls, download YouTube media from the embedded player, mix arbitrary source metadata with unrelated audio, use unlicensed lyric sources, or present a prototype state as completed streaming. These exclusions preserve a maintainable, production-oriented product boundary while still allowing a complete online listening experience to be built through official interfaces.
